WHAT I OFFER

GNSS-Pulse’s mission is to guide technical managers, decision-makers, and teams, helping them understand the complexities of signal physics, the resulting system aspects, and ensure the successful definition, development, and testing of their project through informed choices and the avoidance of unnecessary costs.

Sample of covered fields:

  • GNSS & LEO-PNT system evolution and signal physics.
  • Resilience against jamming, spoofing, and measurement errors. Compatibility analysis.
  • Spaceborne GNSS: Experience in mission definition and the use of spaceborne receivers, particularly on complex topics such as radio occultation, reflectometry, and jamming analysis.
  • Advanced Processing & Resilience: Deep expertise in measurement error mitigation, and signal quality monitoring.
  • Skills building through training tailored to your needs

Whether you are defining a new positioning mission or a new use case, assessing the performance of your positioning system, or seeking high-level independent advice to minimize the risks your upcoming developments, let's connect to secure your project with targeted expertise.

ABOUT ME

After nearly 30 years at CNES shaping the core of GNSS technologies and leveraging their optimal user performance, I founded GNSS-Pulse to bring cutting edge GNSS expertise and independent consulting directly to industrial and institutional teams.

Key achievements:

  • GALILEO: I was the lead inventor who inspired the design of the signals currently deployed on the GALILEO constellation, earning me the 2017 European Inventor Award.
  • Other achievements: I invented the Long Coherent Integration method for Signal Quality Monitoring, as well as the first-ever carrier phase ambiguity resolution in GNSS reflectometry, and inspired numerous research and development initiatives.
